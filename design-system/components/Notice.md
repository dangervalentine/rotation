# Notice

A single italic line on a tinted ground, shown under the action it reports on.

**Consumer provides:** `children` (the message) and `tone`: `warn` (tobacco on camel-soft — something to fix first), `ok` (loden on loden-soft), `neutral` (ink on paper-sunk — "Loaded the sample wardrobe.").

- One sentence, saying what happened and what to do next.
- It is a status region; don't use it for permanent help text (use `.lede`).
